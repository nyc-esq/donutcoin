How it works (30 seconds)
  1. A seller posts an offer, or a buyer posts a request. Everything is signed by the poster’s wallet, so nobody can post as someone else.
  2. Someone takes it. The buyer gets the seller’s Apple Cash or Cash App contact and a trade code; the seller gets told to wait for the money.
  3. The buyer pays in that app with the code in the note, and taps I paid. The seller checks, then sends the DONUT from their wallet with one tap.
  4. When the coins land on the chain, the tracker counts 21 confirmations (about 21 minutes, past the chain’s reorg cap) and then the trade completes itself and both people’s trade counts go up. Turn on notifications in your wallet and your phone buzzes at each step.
  5. The site never holds coins or money. Someone always goes first, so start small with strangers and look at their count.
Not money, not an investment. The site verifies the DONUT leg on the chain and nothing about the payment leg.
