Not money, not an investment, not for sale. Scrypt proof of work, one-minute blocks, 10,000 DONUT a block, never halves. Checkpoints pin history; a heavier chain can replace at most the last 20 blocks; the market settles at 21. 1 Ribbon = 1,000,000 DONUT (100 blocks), 1 Tiara = 10 Ribbons (about a day of the network), 1 Chonk = 10 Tiaras (about a week). Reward spendable after 3 blocks. Refreshes every 5 s.
