The market

The market page is a bulletin board. Users post offers to sell DONUT, or requests to buy it, for Apple Cash or Cash App, and other users take them. The operator does not hold, transfer, escrow or verify any money, does not take part in any trade, and does not verify the identity, honesty or solvency of any user. Every trade is a private arrangement between the two people in it, and either can walk away before the other has performed. The site records that a DONUT transfer appeared on the chain and marks a trade complete once that transfer is 21 blocks deep; it records nothing about the money. The prices shown are computed from users’ own completed trades and a public Dogecoin price feed; they are information, not a quote, an offer or a valuation. Apple Cash and Cash App are their operators’ services under their own terms; use them as those terms allow.
